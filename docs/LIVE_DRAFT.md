# Live draft board

Updated October 3, 2026. Open **Draft → Live draft board**, above Draft Pick Grids.

Migration 00010 and the matching app are deployed. All 158 tests, type checking and the production build passed. Read-only production smoke verified the 1,095-player pool, 104 blank spaces, unchanged reconciled profiles, anonymous write rejection, and desktop/mobile layouts without runtime errors or viewport overflow. The release did not set order, record selections, reveal keepers or open the season.

## Before draft day

The board starts with 13 rounds and eight original slots. Owners stay blank until the commissioner saves a complete order. The existing pick grids below remain available for reviewing pick inventory and history.

Sign in as commissioner and expand **Manage draft order**. If slots have not been published, the editor loads the saved lottery order as an editable suggestion. Existing published slots take precedence when revisiting an assigned board. Adjust the franchises as needed, review the source note, and press **Publish draft order**. Opening the editor, loading the lottery, changing a dropdown and closing/reopening the editor do not publish anything. Edits remain local; if the board revision changes, explicitly load the current order before publishing. Losing the signed-in account, commissioner role or live connection clears the private editor.

The separate [lottery panel](DRAFT_LOTTERY.md) in Commissioner Tools generates the priority for choosing slots. Using that priority as the editor's default is the commissioner's requested starting point; it remains adjustable until submitted. Existing traded picks retain their current owners and original slots. Order changes stop when the commissioner starts the draft and remain locked if selections are later removed.

Eight stable light franchise colors identify teams in the key, assigned slot headings, pick cells and order controls. Slot headings identify the original franchise; each pick cell uses its current owner's color, including traded picks. Names and trade attribution remain visible. Keeper cells retain a purple badge and accent; unassigned slots remain neutral.

Owners and visitors can also expand **Manage draft order** to view the slots. They see a read-only list, with unassigned slots labeled explicitly. Only commissioner accounts receive editing controls; the API and database independently enforce that permission.

Keeper selections remain private until every team's submission is locked. Once keepers are revealed and the order is complete, their payment picks appear purple on the grid. Kept players are greyed out in the player pool, with a purple keeper label, and cannot be selected again. Revealed keeper picks cannot be changed through draft corrections.

The separate [keeper payment editor](KEEPER_READINESS.md#changing-payment-picks-after-the-deadline) lets assigned owners, co-managers and commissioners replace legal payment picks while keeping the retained players fixed, including after the deadline or reveal. Under the **10/14/2026 vote passed** rule, costs are incurred only after the deadline and all participating teams' submissions, using each team's pick inventory at the deadline. Payment corrections must use picks from that inventory that remain owned and available; later acquisitions cannot improve the keeper budget. The editor closes when the commissioner starts the draft, including before the first live selection. A saved post-reveal payment change moves the purple keeper cell, releases the former payment pick when no longer committed, updates used-pick inventory, and advances the draft revision. Boards and draft rosters refresh from that corrected state; stale draft-entry choices must be reloaded.

## Live team rosters

Deployed October 3 with 206 passing tests, a successful production build and desktop/mobile verification of all eight team views. The ESPN settings and eligibility were pulled directly at 2:55 p.m. Eastern time that day.

The live roster panel uses the public draft board to show each team's revealed keepers and recorded selections, with total players and occupied/open ESPN roster slots. It refreshes with the board and follows the current owner of traded picks; corrections and removed selections are reflected in the next board state. Choose a team to inspect its roster. Before publication/reveal, the panel shows the pending state rather than private keeper choices or the previous season's roster.

A fresh programmatic pull from ESPN league 139935, season 2027, on October 3 confirmed **PG ×1, SG ×1, SF ×1, PF ×1, C ×1, G ×1, F ×1, UTIL ×3 and BENCH ×3**: ten active slots and three bench slots. ESPN also configures **IR ×1**, shown separately. The public artifact records the source URLs, capture time and hashes. The player eligibility capture covers all 1,095 draft-catalog players and all 111 frozen keeper-roster players. Slot counts come from `settings.rosterSettings.lineupSlotCounts`; fit uses each player's actual `eligibleSlots`, including flexible positions, instead of inferring them from a primary position.

The suggested arrangement maximizes active-slot coverage and regular roster fit, with stable assignments when possible. It exposes unfilled slots, players without a fit and roster-size excess instead of hiding players. Each player counts once toward the team total. ESPN's configured lineup slots are distinct from per-position roster maximums: the current primary-position limits are unlimited, so a PG slot filled at 1/1 does not mean the team cannot draft another point guard. ESPN describes those settings separately in its [roster-settings guide](https://support.espn.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003902651-Roster-Settings).

IR is not automatically filled or counted as an extra ordinary draft slot. ESPN controls injury eligibility and placement; its [IR guide](https://support.espn.com/hc/en-us/articles/46839854416916-Move-a-Player-On-or-Off-Injured-Reserve-IR-or-Injured-List-IL) explains that only qualifying injury designations can use that slot. The panel is a suggested fit for planning, not a write to ESPN or a finalization of league player ownership. Position eligibility and league settings reflect the dated capture; live polling updates draft selections, not the ESPN settings snapshot.

Refresh the settings and eligibility with `node --import tsx scripts/build-draft-roster-settings.ts --fetch`, using the existing private ESPN environment credentials. Without `--fetch`, the command rebuilds from hash-verified private captures. It only makes GET requests, stores raw responses under ignored `.local/draft-roster-source/`, and publishes allowlisted `data/draft-roster-settings.json` and `data/draft-roster-eligibility.json`. It independently parses ESPN's official client constants for slot names and the unlimited-position sentinel without executing downloaded JavaScript. Wrong seasons, unknown slots, malformed settings and incomplete player coverage stop the build before either public artifact is replaced. The ranking catalog and database selection rules are unchanged.

## Recording and correcting picks

### Starting, pausing and the pick clock

Migration `20261008000021_live_draft_clock.sql` adds a server-authoritative pick clock. Above the board, **START DRAFT** and **Seconds per pick** are visible to everyone and editable only by the commissioner. The default is 90 seconds; the commissioner can save a whole-number duration from 10 to 600 seconds before the first start. Starting requires a complete order and revealed keepers, locks the order and keeper-payment changes, and begins the first available pick. The button becomes **PAUSE DRAFT** while running and **RESUME DRAFT** while paused. Pausing preserves the remaining time; resuming uses that remainder. Each new pick receives the configured duration, skipping keeper cells.

The browser displays the database deadline using server time. It does not choose the timeout player or submit a client timestamp. Expiration takes the first available player in the current pick owner's saved queue, then falls back to the highest available ESPN ROTO rank in the verified catalog. Already kept, drafted or unselectable players are excluded. Unranked players follow ranked players alphabetically. ESPN ranks remain the dated catalog capture described below, not a live ranking feed.

### Team queues

Assigned owners and co-managers can add available players to their team's private draft queue from the player pool, move them up or down, and remove them. Queues are saved to the database and shared with that team's co-managers across devices. Other teams and unassigned commissioners cannot read them. Queue saves use their own revision so another co-manager's changes cannot be silently overwritten. A queue holds up to 100 unique players; it is separate from the existing browser shortlist. Queue priority follows the current owner of traded picks.

Clock controls and picks use the same league lock and board revision. Simultaneous manual picks and expiration requests cannot fill the same slot twice. An expired tick advances one pick and starts a fresh interval; reconnecting after an outage does not consume multiple missed turns. Completing all picks stops the clock. Commissioner corrections reset the current interval, and reopening a completed draft through an undo leaves it paused for review.

Before deploying the updated draft room, apply migration 00021 and verify its `live-draft-clock` Supabase Cron job runs every second. The migration installs the job when `pg_cron` is available and emits a warning or notice if scheduling needs manual setup. Connected boards also request expiration checks; advancing a draft with all browsers closed requires the Cron job. Existing drafts with selection history migrate paused, and applying the migration does not start the league draft. See [database setup](../supabase/README.md#http-routes).

### Recording picks and corrections

Live entry requires a running draft, a complete draft order, revealed keepers, and the `draft_ready` phase. The commissioner can record any current pick; assigned owners and co-managers can record the current pick only when their franchise owns it. Choose an available player from the dropdown or ranking list and select **Record draft pick**. The server accepts only the next available pick in snake order, skipping keeper picks. The board records the franchise that owns the pick, including acquired picks.

Use **Edit** on a recorded selection to replace the player or remove that selection. Both actions require a reason. Removal makes that pick available again; the next entry fills the earliest available pick. Corrections retain the previous selection in the audit record. They do not change frozen keeper eligibility or current roster ownership.

All visible boards refresh regularly and when the page regains focus. A choice remains tied to the pick and revision visible when it was made. If another action changes the board, load the latest pick before submitting. The backend independently rejects stale revisions and duplicate players. An unsuccessful refresh preserves the last visible board and disables draft entry until a successful read.

Trade finalization does not require keeper submissions or a keeper-triggered trading-open timestamp. Migration 00018 removes that prerequisite. The default review period is 24 hours; Commissioner Tools also offers an explicit early-finalization action with a required reason and audit record. Commissioner authorization, ownership checks, keeper commitments and draft protections still apply to either path.

Player trades through the ordinary trade ledger pause once keepers are revealed, including proposals logged before reveal. Those ownership records still describe the pre-draft player pool; they cannot authorize a former owner to transfer a released or re-drafted player. Pick-only trades and recorded obligations retain their existing review and ownership controls. Before the draft starts, a commissioner can include legal payment replacements when finalizing a trade that sends a keeper-committed pick. Each replacement must belong to that team's deadline inventory and remain owned and available after the full trade; receiving a pick outside that inventory cannot make it eligible for keeper payment. Ownership transfers and all replacement allocations are validated and committed atomically. Picks already spent on live draft selections remain unavailable for transfer.

## Player pool and ranking source

The October 3 capture contains 1,095 players from ESPN's 2026–27 league pool, including unsigned and unranked players. ESPN supplies ROTO category ranks for 410; the remaining 685 follow alphabetically. By default, the list and dropdown show the top 200 players matching the selected filters. Searching by name searches the full catalog, including lower-ranked and unranked players; clearing the search returns to the top 200 matches. Search, position, keeper eligibility and availability filters apply to both controls. Keeper eligibility offers All players, Eligible keepers and Ineligible keepers; an unresolved profile appears only under All players. Already-kept players remain disabled when included by an eligibility filter, and Available players only hides them. Source metadata and the league's nine categories accompany the catalog.

The order uses ESPN's supplied `player.draftRanksByRankType.ROTO.rank`, not a newly calculated custom projection. The configured categories are PTS, REB, AST, STL, BLK, 3PM, FG%, FT% and TO. ESPN does not supply a separate verified custom-league weighted ranking in this capture; `rankingSource.customLeagueWeighting` is false. Keeper eligibility comes from the reviewed league profiles, independently of ranking or draft availability. An ineligible keeper can still be drafted.

The player pool is a dated artifact, not a live ESPN ranking feed. Rebuild `data/draft-player-catalog.json` from verified private captures using `scripts/build-team-preferences.ts`. Before deploying a refreshed pool, import the matching reduced `id`, `name`, and `selectable` fields through the database-owner-only `private.import_draft_player_catalog` function, supplying the capture SHA-256 and ESPN season. In the same administrative transaction, update `private.draft_player_rankings` from that artifact with `player_id`, `espn_rank` (null for unranked), `source_sha256` and `source_season`; autodraft trusts only ranks whose source hash and season match the catalog. Omitted players cease to be selectable; previous selections retain their recorded names. Never expose ESPN session credentials or raw member/account data in public artifacts.

## Data and release contract

Migration `20261003000010_live_draft_board.sql` adds the catalog, append-only selection history with explicit voids, season draft revisions, and guarded read/write RPCs. The migration creates no draft selections or order assignments.

`GET /api/draft-board?seasonId=…` returns an allowlisted public board. Before keeper reveal, player and selection fields stay empty; before complete order, pick owners and positions stay empty. `POST /api/draft-board` accepts same-origin, authenticated `set_order`, `record`, `correct`, `undo`, `configure`, `start`, `pause`, and `resume` actions. Commissioner access is required except for an assigned manager recording their own current pick. The separate `tick` action is public and can only apply an already-expired server deadline. The database rechecks authorization after acquiring the league lock and records every mutation with its actor and revision. Direct browser writes to the underlying tables are prohibited.

Domain and PGlite checks cover authorization, privacy, exact catalog correspondence, duplicate players, snake order, traded pick ownership, stale revisions, corrections, and keeper protection. Browser checks use mocked writes for public/setup and commissioner flows, polling conflicts and mobile layouts. A full real-account draft rehearsal belongs in an isolated test season/database; do not reveal the live league merely to test these controls.

The commissioner can explicitly share a read-only lottery replay and separately publish an adjusted draft order. A guided position-choice workflow enforcing whose turn it is remains on the roadmap, along with ESPN draft delegation/transfer tracking, a user-facing correction history, and final roster/season rollover. Filling every pick does not automatically move the season into `in_season`.
## Private commissioner draft and keeper tools

Open **Commissioner Tools → Draft Tool** or **Keeper Suggestions**. Both workspaces require the existing commissioner account. Projection, schedule and saved-rank APIs verify an authenticated user and active commissioner membership; responses are private and never cached. Projection statistics and recommendations never enter the public board, queues, League Lab, or a shared database. The browser saves statistics in the account/season-scoped session draft store, which clears other accounts on account change or sign-out. Supplied BBM ranks are stored separately in a private account-and-season record, containing only ESPN player IDs, ranks and source metadata; other accounts cannot read it.

Each function in **League administration**, **Draft Tool** and **Keeper Suggestions** has an independent disclosure, collapsed initially. Expand only the tools you need; collapsing a section preserves any unfinished form within it.

### Recording a draft trade

Expand **Draft Tool → Record draft trade · players and picks** after keeper finalization and reveal. Choose the two teams, select the players or unspent picks each sends, and enter a trade note. **Review trade** shows the complete exchange; **Record trade now** applies it immediately and records it in the public trade history. The control supports same-year pick swaps and current/next-year exchanges, including 2026 for 2027, alongside drafted players and revealed keepers. It works before the lottery and while the draft is underway.

Player transfers change the live draft roster. Pick transfers preserve their year, original franchise and draft slot. A transferred keeper retains the original payer's spent pick and historical keeper cost. The entire exchange succeeds or fails together after the server rechecks ownership and the board revision. Refresh and review again if the draft or ownership has changed. A running draft clock pauses with its remaining time preserved; resume it from the draft room when ready. A drafted player transferred away must be traded back before its original selection can be corrected or undone.

Revealed keepers populate team rosters and projection comparisons before the lottery, published draft order or first live pick. The pick grid still leaves unpublished slots and pick owners blank. Unrevealed keeper selections remain private.

### Projection imports

- **ESPN:** projections load automatically when you open either strategy tool without a saved ESPN import. The shared session library reuses existing imports; **Refresh ESPN projections** updates them directly, and **Retry ESPN projections** retries an unsuccessful load. Failed automatic loads do not retry repeatedly when navigating. The server uses the existing `ESPN_S2` and `ESPN_SWID` (or `SWID`) connection. Local development needs these in the process environment or `.env.local`; a separate `.env.espn.local` is not automatically loaded by Next. Only the requested season's full-season projected statistics are accepted. Missing projections are not replaced with historical actuals.
- **Yahoo:** projections load automatically through Yahoo's public projected-player table, with a direct refresh control. The source is labeled remaining-season projections. Projected totals are divided by projected games; remaining games are not treated as full-season GP/82 availability. Only the current season and the complete paginated player pool are accepted. Automatic imports can reuse a server-side source read for 30 minutes, retaining its original timestamp; an explicit refresh requests a new read. Failed or incomplete refreshes leave your saved import intact. Yahoo’s published Pre-Season (OR) rank is saved separately from its remaining-season statistics. A CSV/TSV import remains available as a fallback; a ProviderRank column can carry the source rank.
- **Basketball Monster:** export the desired season's projection table and upload CSV/TSV or paste the table. One import in the shared library supplies both Draft Tool and Keeper Suggestions for your account and season in the same browser session. Basketball Monster account access stays with the provider; this app imports its export. Native exports with `first_name`, `last_name`, shooting totals and split rebounds are supported without editing: names are combined, total rebounds and points are calculated, and season totals are converted to per-game values automatically. Provider player IDs are never treated as ESPN IDs. BBM ranks require an explicit Rank, BBMRank or ProviderRank column from the ranked website table. The native Projections.csv file is not sorted by BBM rank, so its row order is never used as rank. Unmatched players keep rank gaps.
- For other CSV/TSV layouts, select **Per game averages** or **Season totals**. Totals require projected GP. Required categories are PTS, REB, AST, STL, BLK, TO, 3PM, FG%, FT%; shooting needs makes and attempts, or percentage plus attempts. Percentage cells containing a makes/attempts pair are supported. A common header is `Player,GP,PTS,REB,AST,STL,BLK,TO,3PM,FGM,FGA,FTM,FTA`.
- IDs must explicitly be ESPN IDs; another provider's generic ID is not treated as ESPN identity. Full names must match uniquely after punctuation/accent normalization. Abbreviated or unmatched names, incomplete statistics, wrong seasons, and duplicate identities are reported. Import full pools for useful keeper comparisons; the keeper model requires at least one complete 13-round league pool.

Older ESPN or Yahoo imports that contain statistics but no provider ranks receive one automatic refresh for the rank migration. A previous import error does not prevent that one attempt. Failed refreshes preserve the saved import and expose a manual retry; navigation does not repeatedly retry the same migration. ESPN's imported ROTO rank takes precedence over the captured catalog rank where supplied, while missing imported ranks retain the catalog fallback.

A rank-bearing BBM import saves its supplied ranks privately to your account and season. Those ranks restore on later visits even if no projection statistics are currently imported. A previously supplied ranked list can be stored in the same private record without storing its projection statistics or adding provider data to the public app bundle. Removing a session projection import does not delete this separately saved rank list. Existing supplied ranks in an imported table take precedence; a conflicting saved list is not mixed into that table's missing ranks.

### Live draft use

Choose your team and a projection source. Picks recorded anywhere in the Croc Bois room refresh every two seconds while the tab is visible; corrections, removals, current pick ownership and revealed keepers rebuild every roster. **Record the next draft pick** explicitly records on the official board with the current revision. It cannot silently submit a selection after that pick changes. Clock controls, corrections and undo remain in the draft room.

**All-team projections** compares overall category win rate, all nine individual category win rates, and average weekly active-slot production across the full season for every team. The separate **Category values** table shows Value and pV, 3V, rV, aV, sV, bV, fg%V, ft%V and toV. These are population z-scores relative to the current teams with complete projection coverage. Counting categories use weekly totals, turnovers reverse the sign, and shooting categories use makes minus the pooled shooting percentage times attempts. Value sums all nine category values. At least two comparable teams are required; equal production scores zero and unavailable estimates remain blank. These are transparent relative scores, not Basketball Monster's proprietary formula. Partial rosters and unequal pick counts affect both tables.

The **Draft rosters → View team** dropdown defaults to your assigned team and lets you inspect every live roster independently. Changing that dropdown leaves the team used for draft recommendations unchanged. Rosters remain available before importing projections and update with the same live board.

**Available player ranks** shows ESPN’s supplied draft rank, BBM’s supplied table or privately saved rank, Yahoo’s published preseason rank, and **ESPN − BBM**. A player ranked ESPN #80 and BBM #45 has +35 places of BBM value. The dropdown sorts by any source rank, biggest BBM value, or schedule/roster fit. Sorting considers the complete available pool before taking 15 players. Missing ranks appear last; source ranks retain drafted players and do not renumber as picks are made. BBM needs a rank-bearing CSV or a saved supplied list; native export row order is never substituted. Player ranks do not require team rosters or forecast statistics. Team win rates, category values and recommendations remain blank when their required data is missing. These draft-table ranks are separate from Keeper Suggestions’ calculated category-value ranks.

**Maximum season category win rate** tests every available projected player against the full-season schedule. A separate **Full-season schedule + roster fit** recommendation adds preferences for position coverage and center depth. The NBA regular-season schedule loads from ESPN automatically; there is no week picker. **Refresh full-season schedule** updates it. **Usable / scheduled games** counts the player’s entire published season: usable games fit into the active lineup with the current roster, while scheduled games are all published games for their NBA team. Counts precede projected-availability scaling.

Full-season analysis assigns players to active slots each day and evaluates Monday–Sunday weeks separately, including partial opening/closing weeks, then averages category win probabilities across the season. It does not pool the season into one matchup. Weeks without NBA games and weekly comparisons with unavailable shooting probabilities are omitted. Daily assignments maximize a nine-category lineup-value heuristic; recommendations do not simulate future picks, streaming or league-specific playoff dates. NBA Cup or makeup games not yet published remain excluded with a warning.

Optional CSV/TSV schedule uploads use date,team, one row per NBA team playing that day, and must cover the full season; the importer infers the date range. If the season schedule cannot be loaded, rank comparisons and rosters remain available while season win rates and recommendations stay unavailable. No single-week or equal-games fallback is displayed as a season forecast.

Source projected GP/82 scales availability when supplied. Team FG% and FT% use total makes divided by total attempts, and turnovers are reversed. Count uncertainty uses overdispersed counts plus a 12% team forecast floor; percentages use binomial uncertainty plus a 1.5-percentage-point floor. These are heuristic estimates, not calibrated odds. **Category win rate** means expected share of nine categories won, averaged equally over comparable opponents and weeks, not probability of winning a matchup. Incomplete rosters stay incomplete; missing player forecasts suppress estimates rather than turning into zero production. Fit scores add explicit roster preferences and are not win probabilities.

### Keeper comparison

All three projection sources appear together, with player/team search and filters for Keep, Don't keep, disagreements, and missing data. Each source is ranked independently using equal-weight nine-category per-game z-scores, attempt-weighted shooting impact, and reversed turnovers. These calculated ranks are distinct from each provider's published rankings; ESPN's supplied draft rank is shown separately. Projected round is rank divided by the number of league teams, rounded up. Round savings are actual payment round minus projected round: round 13 payment for round 6 value is +7.

Suggestions reuse the keeper eligibility rules, current ownership, unused picks and canonical payment allocation. The optimizer selects a jointly legal set maximizing positive total round savings, accounting for competing keepers, traded picks, first-round restrictions and tenure. Confirmed revealed keeper payment reservations can be hypothetically reallocated within the owner's set; ordinary drafted picks cannot. The actual saved payment remains visible separately. These are read-only comparisons even after keeper freeze and never reopen or submit the keeper list. Ownership/pick data refresh every 15 seconds in this view. The per-game keeper model does not model category punts, missed games, or future seasons; zero projected games prevents a Keep recommendation. Missing data or unresolved keeper history is labeled rather than guessed.
